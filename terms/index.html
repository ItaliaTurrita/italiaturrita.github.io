88. Moderation, sanctions and appeals

In plain terms: if a sanction is ever needed, you are given a case number, and you can appeal to someone who was not involved.

8.1 Staff authorised for that purpose may issue warnings, timeouts, kicks and bans. A ban may apply to one Discord server, to selected servers, to Porto Ercole only, or to the whole Community.

8.2 In the case of a serious violation, the Administration may impose a sanction that applies across all of the Community's Discord servers, the Groups and Porto Ercole, and extends to the Linked Account (a "Community-wide Sanction").

8.2A Sanctions shall be proportionate to the nature, seriousness and repetition of the conduct concerned, and shall be imposed only under these Terms and the Community Rules. A Community-wide Sanction is reserved for serious or repeated violations.

8.3 Each moderation action shall be recorded as a Case, the number of which shall be given to you when you are notified of the action.

8.4 Appeals. To appeal, contact the administration of the Repubblica Italiana community and quote the case number. Appeals are reviewed by someone who did not take the original decision. An appeal shall be decided by a member of Staff other than the one who took the original decision, who shall consider it fairly and give you a reasonable opportunity to explain the circumstances. You shall be informed of the outcome.

8.5 Nothing in these Terms authorises any sanction or decision that discriminates unlawfully, or that would override a protection that the law gives you.
