If you do not agree, you must not use the Service.

2. The Service

The Service supports the Italia roleplay community across its Discord servers, its Roblox groups and the Roblox experience Porto Ercole. It:

  • links Discord accounts to Roblox accounts, and assigns Discord roles and nicknames that reflect Roblox group ranks;
  • administers moderation in the community's Discord servers and in Porto Ercole, and keeps a record of each moderation action;
  • handles warrant applications between Porto Ercole and the Sistema Giudiziario server;
  • manages Roblox group ranks, join requests, points, medals and roleplay positions for the community's groups.

The Service is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc. or Roblox Corporation.

3. Eligibility

You must be at least the minimum age required by Discord and Roblox in your country of residence, and you must comply with the Discord Terms of Service, the Discord Community Guidelines and the Roblox Terms of Use at all times.

4. Account linking

  • You may link only a Roblox account that you own and control. You must not link, or attempt to link, an account belonging to another person, and you must not help another person link your account.
  • The verification link the Service gives you is personal to your Discord account. Do not share it.
  • You may link a different Roblox account at any time with /account change. The previously linked account is then no longer synchronised and, where applicable, returns to the citizen rank of the main group; any roleplay positions it held are referred for review.
  • You may request erasure of your data at any time with /privacy forget, as described in section 9.

6. Staff permissions and oversight

Certain members are granted permissions to manage ranks, points, medals and moderation through the Service. All such actions are recorded. The Service automatically monitors staff activity for signs of misuse, including excessive rank changes, changes to one's own rank, actions against people of equal or higher rank and repeated reversals. When such activity is detected, the action is blocked and the member's permissions within the Service are suspended pending review by the administration, which may reverse any changes made.

7. Moderation and sanctions

  • Authorised staff may issue warnings, timeouts, kicks and bans. A ban may apply to one Discord server, to chosen servers, to Porto Ercole only, or to the whole community.
  • For serious violations, the administration may impose a community-wide sanction, which applies across all of the community's Discord servers, Roblox groups and Porto Ercole, and extends to the linked account.
  • Each moderation action is recorded as a case with a case number, which is given to you when you are told about it.
  • Appeals. To appeal, contact the administration of the Italia Turrita community and quote the case number. Appeals are reviewed by someone who did not take the original decision. An appeal is decided by a member of staff other than the one who took the original decision, and you will be told the outcome.

8. Roleplay ranks, positions and virtual items

Ranks, positions, points and medals exist solely within the community's roleplay. They confer no ownership rights and have no monetary or other value outside the community. They may be granted, changed or withdrawn by the people authorised to manage them.

You may link again and request to rejoin afterwards.
