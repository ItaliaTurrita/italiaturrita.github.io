In short

  • We keep the link between your Discord and Roblox accounts, your ranks and records in the community, and moderation records.
  • We never get your password, your messages, your email or your payment details.
  • You can see what we hold with /privacy view, and have it erased with /privacy forget.
  • Nothing about you is posted anywhere public; staff records are only visible to staff.
  • If a decision about you was made automatically, you can ask a person to look at it again.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Italia Turrita (the "Service") collects, uses, stores and deletes personal data, and the rights you have over that data. The Service is operated by the administration of the Italia Turrita community, which acts as the data controller ("we", "us", "our").

The Service does not read your messages, other than posts made in the warrant-application forum, and it does not collect your email address, IP address or payment information.

3. How and why we use your data

  • To provide the Service (performance of the service you request): account linking, roles and nicknames, warrants, ranks, points, medals and positions.
  • To keep the community safe (our legitimate interests): moderation, the prevention of ban evasion and alternate accounts, and the protection of the community's groups against misuse of staff permissions.
  • To keep accurate records (our legitimate interests): the staff activity log, which allows mistakes and misuse to be identified and reversed.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling for marketing.

4. Automated decisions

Some decisions are made automatically:

  • join requests to the community's main group are checked against public Roblox profile information: the account must be at least 30 days old and have at least 10 badges. Requests that pass may be accepted automatically; requests that do not pass are referred to a human reviewer and are never declined automatically;
  • join requests to the Vigili del Fuoco group are accepted automatically when the account owns the department's game pass, and are declined automatically after 48 hours if it does not;
  • staff activity that matches the misuse rules suspends the member's permissions within the Service until an administrator reviews the case.

You may ask for any automated decision that affects you to be reviewed by a person, and give your point of view, by contacting us (see section 8). A person who was not involved in the original decision will review it.

5. Where your data is stored and who receives it

Your data is processed on our behalf by these processors, each under its data processing agreement:

  • Cloudflare, Inc., which runs the Service and holds short-lived working data;
  • GitHub, Inc., which hosts the Service's data store in a private repository;
  • Google LLC, which hosts the organisation roster spreadsheets.

Discord and Roblox are not our processors: they are independent controllers of the data on their own platforms, under their own privacy policies. Roblox also holds the data that Porto Ercole needs, such as bans and medals.

These providers may process data in the United States. Transfers from the UK and the EU rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum.

Moderation cases, roster changes and warrant decisions are posted only in staff and log channels that ordinary members cannot see. Nothing about you is published on a public page.

7. Erasure (right to be forgotten)

You may request erasure at any time with /privacy forget. You can do so once every 24 hours. On confirmation:

  • your account link and the linked Discord roles are removed immediately;
  • you are removed from the community's Roblox groups, because ranks and positions cannot be maintained without the records that manage them;
  • your points, positions and roster entries are deleted, and your Roblox user ID and name are removed from warrant records;
  • if you choose, Porto Ercole is asked to erase your game data as well;
  • you receive a direct message with a reference number when the process is complete, usually within minutes. We keep a record that the erasure took place, which does not contain your identifiers.

Deleted data also leaves the history of our data store: the history is replaced with a single current copy every day, so erased and expired data is gone from it within about 48 hours.

The following data is retained after an erasure request, because it is necessary to enforce sanctions and to prevent ban evasion: moderation cases, blocklist entries and misuse reviews. It is deleted when its retention period ends (730 days). The staff activity log and join-request checks are deleted at the end of their retention periods (90 and 30 days respectively). Messages already posted in Discord channels are not edited by an erasure; you may ask the administration to remove them.

When Roblox forwards an erasure request concerning a Porto Ercole player, Porto Ercole erases its game data and the Service erases its data about that Roblox account in the same way.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, gives you the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you; a summary is available at any time with /privacy view;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, as described in section 7;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, the processing of your data;
  • ask for an automated decision to be reviewed by a person;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the Italia Turrita administration on the community's Discord servers. We will respond within one month.

9. Security

Access credentials for the Service are held in Cloudflare's encrypted secret store and are never kept in its code or data store. The data store is a private repository. Access to staff functions is limited by role and monitored for misuse.

10. Children

You must be at least the minimum age required by Discord and Roblox in your country of residence to use the Service. Porto Ercole is intended for players aged 16 and over. Because some members may be young, we keep to a few rules: we collect only what the Service needs, we never publish anything about members, we never use data for advertising, staff records stay in staff-only channels, and the summary at the top of this policy explains what we do in plain words. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us.
