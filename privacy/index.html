Italia Turrita Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

✓In short

  • The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We are based in South Africa, and South African law (POPIA) applies.
  • We keep the link between your Discord and Roblox accounts, your ranks and records in the community, and moderation records.
  • We never get your password, your messages, your email or your payment details.
  • You can see what we hold with /privacy view, and have it erased with /privacy forget or from the dashboard.
  • Nothing about you is posted anywhere public; staff records are only visible to staff.
  • If a decision about you was made automatically, you can ask a person to look at it again.
  • Every record has a time limit, after which it is deleted. If you have a question, please ask us; a person will answer.

11. Introduction, definitions and interpretation

In plain terms: this Policy explains, carefully and completely, what happens to your information and who is responsible for it.

1.1 This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how Italia Turrita (the "Service") collects, uses, stores and deletes Personal Data, and the rights you have in respect of it. It forms part of, and shall be read together with, the Italia Turrita Terms of Service (the "Terms").

1.2 The Service is operated by the administration of the Italia Turrita community, which acts as the controller of the Personal Data described in this Policy (the "Administration", "we", "us", "our"), and as the responsible party for the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 of the Republic of South Africa ("POPIA"). The Service is operated from the Republic of South Africa, where its responsible party is domiciled, and POPIA accordingly applies to all of its processing. Requests concerning Personal Data may be sent by email to repubblicaitaliana-rblx@googlegroups.com, or through the Italia Turrita administration on the community's Discord servers. The identity of the individual responsible is disclosed on request to any data subject or supervisory authority.

1.2A Because the Service is offered to Members in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (together, the "GDPR") also apply to the processing of their Personal Data. The contact mailbox is shared with the administration of the Community, including a co-owner of the Groups resident in Italy, who may receive requests and pass them to the responsible party but does not operate the Service or have access to its Data Store.

1.3 In this Policy, unless the context otherwise requires:

  • (a) "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, and "processing" has the meaning given to it by the data protection law applicable to you;
  • (b) "Controller" means a person who determines the purposes and means of processing, and "Processor" means a person who processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller;
  • (c) "Data Store" means the private GitHub repository, on its data branch, in which the Service keeps its records;
  • (d) "Platforms" means Discord and Roblox;
  • (e) "Member", "Linked Account", "Groups", "Porto Ercole", "Staff", "Staff Permissions", "Case" and "Dashboard" have the meanings given to them in the Terms; and
  • (f) "you" and "your" refer to the person whose Personal Data is processed.

1.4 Where this Policy and the Terms conflict on any matter concerning Personal Data, this Policy shall prevail, subject always to any mandatory provision of the data protection law applicable to you.

44. Automated decision-making

In plain terms: a few things happen automatically, and you can always ask a person to look at them again.

4.1 The following decisions are taken by automated means:

  • (a) join requests to the Community's main group are checked against public Roblox profile information: the account must be at least 30 days old and hold at least 10 badges. A request that meets these criteria may be accepted automatically; a request that does not is referred to a human reviewer and is never declined automatically;
  • (b) join requests to the Vigili del Fuoco group are accepted automatically where the account owns the department's game pass, and are declined automatically after 48 hours where it does not; and
  • (c) Staff activity that matches the misuse rules suspends the Member's Staff Permissions within the Service until an administrator has reviewed the matter.

4.1A These decisions are made in accordance with Article 22 of the GDPR and section 71 of POPIA. Join-request checks apply only the published criteria above. A detection of possible misuse of Staff Permissions is an indication, not a finding: it is recorded with the activity that caused it, and it is reviewed by an administrator without undue delay. The Member concerned is told the case number and the outcome.

4.2 You are entitled to have any automated decision affecting you reviewed by a person, to express your point of view and to contest the decision, by contacting us in accordance with clause 12. The review shall be carried out by a person who was not involved in the original decision.

In plain terms: three service providers help us run things, Discord and Roblox answer for their own platforms, and nothing about you is ever made public.

5.1 Personal Data is processed on our behalf by the following Processors, each under its data processing agreement:

  • (a) Cloudflare, Inc., which runs the Service and holds short-lived working data;
  • (b) GitHub, Inc., which hosts the Data Store in a private repository; and
  • (c) Google LLC, which hosts the organisation roster spreadsheets.

5.2 Discord and Roblox are not our Processors. They are independent Controllers of the data on their own platforms, under their own privacy policies. Roblox also holds the data that Porto Ercole requires, such as bans and medals.

5.3 These providers may process Personal Data in the United States. Transfers from the United Kingdom and the European Union rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses together with the UK addendum. Transfers of Personal Data outside the Republic of South Africa are made under section 72 of POPIA, on the basis of binding agreements that provide an adequate level of protection.

5.4 Moderation Cases, roster changes and warrant decisions are posted only in Staff and log channels that ordinary Members cannot see. Nothing about you is published on any public page.

77. Erasure (right to be forgotten)

In plain terms: you can ask for your data to be erased whenever you wish; a few safety records stay until their time runs out.

7.1 You may request erasure at any time by means of /privacy forget or the Dashboard, once in every period of 24 hours. Upon your confirmation:

  • (a) your account link and the linked Discord roles are removed immediately;
  • (b) you are removed from the Groups, because ranks and positions cannot be maintained without the records that manage them;
  • (c) your points, positions and roster entries are deleted, and your Roblox user ID and name are removed from warrant records;
  • (d) if you so choose, Porto Ercole is asked to erase your game data as well; and
  • (e) you receive a direct message bearing a reference number when the process is complete, usually within minutes.

7.2 We keep a record that the erasure took place, which does not contain your identifiers. Erased data leaves the history of the Data Store as described in clause 6.2.

7.3 Notwithstanding clause 7.1, the following data is retained after an erasure request, because it is necessary to enforce sanctions and to prevent ban evasion: moderation Cases, blocklist entries and misuse reviews. That data is deleted when its retention period ends (730 days, or for as long as a ban remains in force, if longer). The staff activity log and join-request checks are deleted at the end of their own retention periods (90 and 30 days respectively).

7.4 Messages already posted in Discord channels are not edited by an erasure; you may ask the Administration to remove them.

7.5 Where Roblox forwards an erasure request concerning a Porto Ercole player, Porto Ercole erases its game data and the Service erases its data concerning that Roblox account in the same manner.

7.6 After an erasure you remain welcome to link an account again and to request to rejoin the Groups.

88. Your rights

In plain terms: this information is about you, so you have a say in what happens to it.

8.1 Under POPIA (in particular sections 5, 23, 24 and 71) and, where it applies to you, the GDPR, you are entitled to:

  • (a) access the Personal Data we hold about you; a summary is available at any time with /privacy view;
  • (b) have inaccurate data corrected;
  • (c) have your data erased, as described in clause 7;
  • (d) object to, or ask us to restrict, the processing of your data;
  • (e) have an automated decision reviewed by a person, as described in clause 4.2; and
  • (f) receive a copy of your Personal Data on request; and
  • (g) lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where an alleged infringement took place.

8.1A The supervisory authority for the Service is the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za). Members in the European Union or the United Kingdom may also complain to their own authority, including the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy (garanteprivacy.it), the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom (ico.org.uk), and the data protection authority of any other member state of the European Union in which you live.

8.2 To exercise any of these rights, contact us by email to repubblicaitaliana-rblx@googlegroups.com, or through the Italia Turrita administration on the community's Discord servers. We shall respond within one month, and may ask for information that confirms your identity before doing so. Exercising your rights is free of charge.

99. Security

In plain terms: access to your information is locked down and watched.

9.1 Access credentials for the Service are held in Cloudflare's encrypted secret store and are never kept in its code or in the Data Store.

9.2 The Data Store is a private repository. Access to Staff functions is limited by role and monitored for misuse, and Dashboard sessions expire 12 hours after sign-in.

9.2A In the event of a breach of security that affects Personal Data, we shall assess and contain it without delay. We shall notify the competent supervisory authority, and the persons affected, where and within the time that the law requires (Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR; section 22 of POPIA).

9.3 The Dashboard uses a single strictly necessary session cookie and stores your language preference in your browser. Details are set out in the Italia Turrita Cookie Policy.

1010. Age restriction and children

In plain terms: the Service is for adults only. If we learn that someone under 18 has signed up, we remove their data.

10.1 The Service is offered only to persons aged 18 years of age or over. Under POPIA, a person under 18 is a child, and the personal information of children may be processed only in the circumstances set out in sections 34 and 35 of POPIA. The Service does not seek or rely on the consent of a parent or guardian, and the parental controls and consents of Roblox and Discord apply only to those platforms' own processing.

10.1A Each Member confirms that they are aged 18 years of age or over when they link an account or sign in to the Dashboard. If we learn that a Member is under 18 years of age, we unlink their account and erase their Personal Data in accordance with clause 7.

10.1B Porto Ercole is a Roblox experience that is subject to Roblox's own age rules. Where Staff take moderation action in Porto Ercole concerning a player who is not a Member, the Service records only the player's Roblox user ID and name and the moderation Case.

10.2 In addition, we keep to the following safeguards:

  • (a) we collect only what the Service needs;
  • (b) we never publish anything about Members;
  • (c) we never use Personal Data for advertising;
  • (d) Staff records stay in Staff-only channels; and
  • (e) the summary at the top of this Policy explains what we do in plain words.

10.3 A parent or guardian who believes that a person under 18 years of age has used the Service may contact us in accordance with clause 12, and we shall act on the matter under clause 10.1A.
