Italia Turrita Cookie Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

✓In short

  • The Service sets one cookie, and only when you sign in to the Dashboard. It keeps you signed in and is deleted when you sign out or after 12 hours.
  • That cookie is strictly necessary. It is not used for analytics, advertising or tracking, and no consent banner is required for it.
  • The Dashboard remembers your chosen language in your browser's local storage. Nothing else is stored on your device.
  • The public pages, including this one, set no cookies at all.

11. Introduction and scope

In plain terms: this Policy lists everything the Service stores on your device, and why.

1.1 This Cookie Policy (the "Cookie Policy") explains how Italia Turrita (the "Service") uses cookies and similar technologies on its web pages and on the Dashboard. The Service is operated by the administration of the Repubblica Italiana community (the "Administration", "we", "us", "our"). This Cookie Policy forms part of, and shall be read together with, the Italia Turrita Terms of Service (the "Terms") and the Italia Turrita Privacy Policy (the "Privacy Policy").

1.2 In this Cookie Policy:

  • (a) a "cookie" is a small text file that a website places on your device and that your browser returns to that website on later requests;
  • (b) "local storage" is a facility of your browser that allows a website to keep a small amount of information on your device, which is not sent to the website automatically;
  • (c) "strictly necessary" describes storage without which a service that you have expressly requested cannot be provided; and
  • (d) terms defined in the Terms or the Privacy Policy have the same meaning here.

22. Cookies set by the Service

In plain terms: one cookie, for signing in, and nothing else.

2.1 The Service sets the following cookie, and no other:

NamePurposeDurationType
it_adminKeeps you signed in to the Dashboard after you sign in with Discord. It holds a random session identifier only; it contains no name, identifier or other Personal Data.12 hours from sign-in, or until you sign outStrictly necessary; first party

2.2 The it_admin cookie is set only when you sign in to the Dashboard. It is marked HttpOnly (it cannot be read by scripts), Secure (it is sent only over encrypted connections) and SameSite=Lax (it is not sent with requests started by other websites), and it is limited to the /admin path. It is deleted when you sign out, and the session it refers to is deleted from our records when it expires.

2.3 The Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy pages, the account-linking pages and every other public page of the Service set no cookies.

33. Local storage

In plain terms: the Dashboard remembers whether you prefer English or Italian.

3.1 The Dashboard keeps the following item in your browser's local storage:

KeyPurposeDuration
it.langRemembers the language you selected for the Dashboard (English or Italian).Until you clear your browser's site data

3.2 This item is not sent to us and contains no Personal Data. If local storage is unavailable, the Dashboard is shown in English.

44. Third parties

In plain terms: other companies' content shown on our pages follows their own policies.

4.1 The Service does not place cookies on behalf of any third party and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking.

4.2 Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and, on the Dashboard, profile images from Discord's content network. Your browser contacts those providers directly to fetch them, which discloses your IP address to them; the Service does not ask them to set cookies.

4.3 When you sign in with Discord or link a Roblox account, you are taken to Discord or Roblox. Any cookies those websites set are governed by their own cookie and privacy policies, not by this Cookie Policy.

55. Legal basis

In plain terms: the law allows strictly necessary storage without asking first.

5.1 The it_admin cookie and the it.lang item are strictly necessary to provide the Dashboard that you have requested, and are therefore exempt from the requirement to obtain consent under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC as implemented in the law applicable to you, and under the equivalent rules elsewhere. Any Personal Data connected with a Dashboard session is processed as described in the Privacy Policy.

5.2 The Service will not introduce any cookie or similar technology that is not strictly necessary without first amending this Cookie Policy and, where the law requires it, obtaining your consent.

66. Managing cookies

In plain terms: you can delete them at any time; you will simply be signed out.

6.1 You may delete or block cookies and local storage through your browser's settings at any time. If you block the it_admin cookie, you will not be able to sign in to the Dashboard; every other function of the Service remains available through its commands.

77. Changes and contact

In plain terms: if anything here changes, the date at the top changes too.

7.1 We may amend this Cookie Policy from time to time. The effective date shown at the top identifies the current version.

7.2 Questions about this Cookie Policy may be sent by email to repubblicaitaliana-rblx@googlegroups.com, or through the Repubblica Italiana administration on the community's Discord servers.
